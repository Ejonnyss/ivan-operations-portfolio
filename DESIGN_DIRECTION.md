# Portfolio visual direction

- Audience: hiring managers for Operations, Implementation, Product Operations and AI delivery. The first screen should signal judgment, clarity and real work.
- Direction: editorial minimalism with an operations log motif. Case numbers and small evidence labels form the signature detail.
- Palette: ink `#17231F`, warm paper `#F6F3EC`, rust `#B54F35`, subdued green `#66746C`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Type: Iowan Old Style / Georgia for measured editorial headings; Avenir Next / Arial for interface text. Native font stacks avoid third-party requests and remain fast.
- Rhythm: 8 px base, 1120 px content width, mobile-first layout, one strong headline per screen. A quiet ruled grid replaces decorative stock imagery.
- Interaction: restrained 180 ms states, keyboard focus, no scroll effects or animation dependency.

The site shows only verified work. A synthetic KadrLine sandbox is explicitly labelled as a simulation; the Life screenshot comes from the public demo.
